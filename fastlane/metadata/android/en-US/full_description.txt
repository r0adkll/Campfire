<p><b>Campfire</b> is an unofficial, feature-rich native client for <a href="https://www.audiobookshelf.org/">Audiobookshelf</a> — the self-hosted audiobook and podcast server.</p>

<p>Connect your server and enjoy your library through a feature rich experience on the go</p>

<p>Features:</p>
<ul>
<li>Browse, filter and search your libraries: books, series, collections, playlists, authors, shows, and more</li>
<li>Stream audiobooks from your server, or download them for offline listening</li>
<li>Listening progress synced with your server and multiple devices in real time</li>
<li>Android Auto support for a hands-free experience while driving</li>
<li>Homescreen widget for controlling playback on the fly</li>
<li>Landscape, foldables, and large-screen supported natively</li>
<li>Listening stats: track your sessions, days listened, and minutes played</li>
<li>Sleep timer with shake-to-reset</li>
<li>OIDC authentication supported</li>
<li>Multiple accounts and servers, with fast switching</li>
<li>Content dynamically themes based on cover image</li>
<li>Personalized theming with a selection of built-in themes or the ability to customize your own</li>
</ul>

<p>Campfire requires a self-hosted Audiobookshelf server to connect to. Visit audiobookshelf.org to learn how to set one up.</p>
<p>This is a third-party client and is not affiliated with the Audiobookshelf project.</p>
