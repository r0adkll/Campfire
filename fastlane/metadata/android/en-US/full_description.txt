<p><b>Campfire</b> is an unofficial, native client for <a href="https://www.audiobookshelf.org/">Audiobookshelf</a> — the self-hosted audiobook and podcast server. It is built with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose for a fast, modern experience that feels at home on your device.</p>

<p><b>You need your own Audiobookshelf server to use this app.</b></p>

<p>Features:</p>
<ul>
<li>Browse your libraries: books, series, collections, playlists, and authors</li>
<li>Stream audiobooks from your server, or download them for offline listening</li>
<li>Listening progress synced with your server in real time</li>
<li>Android Auto support</li>
<li>Listening stats: track your sessions, days listened, and minutes played</li>
<li>Sign in with username/password or OpenID Connect (OIDC)</li>
<li>Multiple accounts and servers, with fast switching</li>
<li>Personalized theming with Material color palettes, light and dark modes</li>
</ul>

<p>Campfire is a work in progress and is not affiliated with the Audiobookshelf project.</p>
