Campfire is an unofficial, feature-rich native client for Audiobookshelf, the self-hosted audiobook and podcast server.

Connect your server and enjoy your library on the go.

Features:

* Browse, filter and search your libraries: books, series, collections, playlists, authors, shows, and more
* Stream audiobooks from your server, or download them for offline listening
* Listening progress synced with your server and other devices in real time
* Android Auto support for a hands-free experience while driving
* Home screen widget for controlling playback on the fly
* Landscape, foldables, and large screens supported natively
* Listening stats: track your sessions, days listened, and minutes played
* Sleep timer with shake-to-reset
* OIDC authentication supported
* Multiple accounts and servers, with fast switching
* Content dynamically themed from cover art
* Personalized theming with a selection of built-in themes or the ability to customize your own

Campfire requires a self-hosted Audiobookshelf server to connect to. Visit audiobookshelf.org to learn how to set one up.

This is a third-party client and is not affiliated with the Audiobookshelf project.
